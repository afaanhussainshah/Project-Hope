### Starc's new-ball records (cricket.com.au, Nov 2026) ✅

- **4 wickets with the first ball of a Test match.** World record; first bowler ever to do it.
- **28 first-over Test wickets**, second all-time behind Anderson's 29. Say "second", because one report claims he's passed Anderson.
- 9 first-ball dismissals (either innings), the most ever.
- 8 of his first-over wickets came in day-night Tests, versus 9 for every other bowler combined (ESPNcricinfo, 14 Dec 2025).
- Fastest ball in Test cricket: 160.4 km/h (fun fact).

### Bumrah's extras ✅

- Best average of anyone at 200 Test wickets: 19.56 (Dec 2024). First ever to reach 200 wickets for under 4,000 runs.
- New ball in Australia 2024-25: first-20-overs average **10.6** (Wisden, 25 Jul 2025).

### World Cups

- Starc: **27 wickets at the 2019 ODI World Cup**, the record for a single tournament. Fastest to 50 World Cup wickets (19 matches) ✅.
- Bumrah: 20 wickets at economy 4.06 in 2023 ⚠️ (secondary source).
