“11 Players. 1 Season. Then Gone.” (Full Script, Afridi as Main Character)

Format: Vertical 9:16 | Theme: Paper cut-out, stop-motion style | Length: ~35 seconds

Look: Off-white paper texture, hand-cut shapes with rough edges, soft drop shadows for depth, and choppy 8-12 fps movement. Palette: IPL blue, Pakistan green, Rajasthan pink, gold, cream, and charcoal for the dark moment.

SCENE 1 | 0:00-0:03 | HOOK

VISUAL: A white paper sheet rips away to show a large Shahid Afridi cut-out in Deccan Chargers colors, with 10 smaller green figures behind him. A hand slides a “2009” tag over them, and the small figures are cut away one by one. Afridi leaves last. Text on a torn strip: “2008: AFRIDI + 10 OTHERS. 2009: ZERO.”
SOUND: Loud paper rip, then rapid scissor snips.

VOICEOVER:
In the first IPL, Shahid Afridi and ten other Pakistani players took the field. Then they all vanished. Here’s why.

SCENE 2 | 0:03-0:12 | THE GOLDEN SEASON

VISUAL: A layered paper IPL stadium pops up with a bobbing cut-out crowd. AFRIDI slides in first, large and centered, labeled “DECCAN CHARGERS.” Then smaller TANVIR, YOUNIS, AKMAL figures slide in wearing Rajasthan pink. A small gold paper trophy rises over the pink figures. Text: “IPL 2008: PAKISTAN’S ONLY SEASON”
SOUND: Crowd murmur, paper shuffles, a light “pop” on each name.

VOICEOVER:
2008. The first IPL. Shahid Afridi, Pakistan’s biggest star, played for Deccan Chargers. Sohail Tanvir, Younis Khan and Kamran Akmal were there too. And the three of them won the trophy with Rajasthan Royals.

SCENE 3 | 0:12-0:22 | THE TURNING POINT

VISUAL: The bright paper layers fold shut like a book. A charcoal paper layer slides across with a paper moon and a plain skyline silhouette of Mumbai. Text: “NOVEMBER 2008.” Keep it still and quiet, with no recreation of the attacks. A “2009” tag slides in and the player row appears empty.
SOUND: Music drops to near-silence, with one slow paper fold.

VOICEOVER:
Then came the Mumbai attacks in November 2008. Relations between the two countries collapsed. And when the 2009 IPL came around, no Pakistani players were picked.

SCENE 4 | 0:22-0:30 | THE “BAN”

VISUAL: A paper auction paddle and a scrolling list of blank name strips. A paper hand stamps “NEVER OFFICIALLY BANNED” in red. Then a single figure labeled AZHAR MAHMOOD, holding a tiny paper British passport, slides in as the only exception.
SOUND: Stamp thud, light paper flick.

VOICEOVER:
Here’s the strange part. Officials have said teams make their own decisions. No Pakistani player has featured since, and the only exception was Azhar Mahmood, who played on a British passport.

SCENE 5 | 0:30-0:35 | CLOSE

VISUAL: A single empty paper jersey hangs on a paper hanger and swings gently. Text on a torn strip: “Should they return? 👇”
SOUND: Soft piano note, then silence.

VOICEOVER:
Almost two decades on, the question hasn’t gone away. Should Pakistani players ever return to the IPL?
