# How to Build a Cricket Pitch (Vertical 9:16 Reel)

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 | **Tone:** Mysterious, satisfying, cinematic | **Length:** ~40 seconds

## 0:00–0:03 | HOOK
- **VISUAL:** Extreme close-up of a spade slamming into the dirt. Cut to a hand pointing at the ground. Bold text: `DIG HERE. TRUST ME.`
- **VOICEOVER (low, intriguing):**
  > Grab a spade. Dig right here. In a few steps, this dirt will make a world-class bowler's day... or ruin it.

## 0:03–0:12 | THE DIG
- **VISUAL:** Time-lapse of digging a long rectangular trench. Text: `22 YARDS. ONE FOOT DEEP.`
- **VOICEOVER:**
  > Mark out twenty-two yards. Dig down about a foot. This is the foundation nobody ever sees.

## 0:12–0:22 | THE LAYERS
- **VISUAL:** Cross-section animation: gravel, then sand, then clay soil, each layer dropping in. A roller compacts the top.
- **VOICEOVER:**
  > Fill it in layers. Gravel for drainage. Sand to level. Then heavy clay on top, rolled until it's rock solid. That clay decides the bounce.

## 0:22–0:32 | THE LIFE
- **VISUAL:** Grass seeds scattered, water spraying, sun time-lapse, a mower trimming close.
- **VOICEOVER:**
  > Seed the grass. Water it. Roll it. Mow it. More grass means more seam. Shave it close and it starts to turn.

## 0:32–0:40 | THE PAYOFF
- **VISUAL:** Paint the creases, hammer in the stumps, place the bails. Slow golden-hour reveal. Freeze frame. Text: `Now call your friends.`
- **VOICEOVER (warm, proud):**
  > Paint the creases. Plant the stumps. Congratulations. You've built a pitch.
