One piece of
Sandpaper
Shredded
A nation's reputation
Cape Town
2018
Cameras caught
Cameron Bancroft
Rubbing the ball
With something yellow
Then desperately hiding it
He said it was
Sticky tape.
It was sandpaper.
David Warner
Had devised the plan.
And captain Steve Smith
Knew about it
But failed to stop it.
The punishment?
Smith and Warner:
Twelve months banned.
Bancroft:
Nine months.
Australia lost the series
3–1.
But the bigger loss...
Was its reputation.
